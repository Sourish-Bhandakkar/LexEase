Risk Level Guidelines (always from the customer's perspective):
- high: The customer faces one-sided or uncapped liability, major loss of remedies, a burdensome dispute process, an automatic renewal trap, or significant financial penalties with little or no recourse.
- medium: The customer has obligations with limited protection, faces meaningful ambiguity, or accepts restrictions that may be inconvenient but are not clearly severe.
- low: The term is clear, reasonably balanced for the customer, and creates no unusual customer-side exposure.

Recommendation Guidelines:
- high: Recommend negotiating the clause to add caps/notice periods, or consulting a legal advisor.
- medium: Recommend clarifying ambiguous language, requesting a time limit or cap, or balancing terms before signing.
- low: Recommend reviewing for completeness but indicate the clause is generally acceptable as written.
